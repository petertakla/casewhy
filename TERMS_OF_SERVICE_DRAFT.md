## 2. What CaseWhy Is

CaseWhy helps you track a pending U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) case. You provide a receipt number; we check its status against USCIS's own Case Status API and USCIS's FOIA API, and use an AI layer to translate that status into a plain-language explanation and general next steps, grounded in a curated set of public USCIS policy memos, processing-time data, and case-law history. Depending on your plan, the Service may also offer AI chat about your case, tracking for multiple cases (e.g., family members'), document storage, and an escalation toolkit that helps you look up your congressional representative and draft your own inquiry letters when a case appears delayed.

**CaseWhy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by USCIS, the Department of Homeland Security, or any other government agency.** We display information sourced from public government systems; we do not control that data and cannot guarantee its accuracy, completeness, or timeliness.
