Boilerplate — third person

CaseWhy is a free, plain-language explainer of USCIS case status. It tracks a case by receipt number, explains what each status means with the USCIS source cited, shows published processing times and the Visa Bulletin, flags a case that has waited longer than USCIS's own published time, and lists free legal help — accredited representatives, legal aid, pro bono representation, and attorneys — by state. It is available in English and Spanish, carries no ads, and is not a law firm. casewhy.com

Boilerplate — first person (for partners quoting us)

"We built CaseWhy because our own family's naturalization cases went quiet after the interview and nobody could tell us why. It explains what a USCIS status means in plain language, cites the source, and shows you the formal channels when a case is genuinely overdue. It's free, it has no ads, and it never gives legal advice — for that, it points you to the right kind of help." — Peter Takla, founder
