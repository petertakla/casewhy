For elder-law & legal aid attorneys

CaseWhy Appeals is the Medicare appeals engine that works alongside what you already use — Clio, LegalServer, MyCase, or your own file system. It's not a referral service: no fee, no exclusivity, ever.

CaseWhy Appeals is not affiliated with or endorsed by Medicare, CMS, or any health plan, and is not a law firm.

Seats

  • Professional seat, private practice. Free through the founding pilot (to February 28, 2027), then $99 a month, unlimited cases.
  • Free seat, permanently. For legal aid organizations, with attribution, forever.

The higher-level work

  • The ALJ hearing request (OMHA-100) filled, not just described, at level 3.
  • The Medicare Appeals Council review request letter at level 4, citing 42 CFR Part 405, Subpart I's own hearing and Council timelines.
  • The good-cause package when a deadline was missed for a real reason — a letter, not a bare form, built from your client's own facts.
  • Medicare Appeals Council decisions in the underlying knowledge corpus, cited by letters where they apply — not just the regulation text.

The DAB-101 request for Council review of an ALJ decision is referenced, with a link to CMS's own form; CaseWhy Appeals doesn't fill it yet.
