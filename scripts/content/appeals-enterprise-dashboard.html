What leadership sees

The four figures the board is built around.

Open appeals

Every case in progress, by facility and by region.

Deadlines approaching

Cases where the filing window closes soon, calculated from the notice rather than entered by hand.

Unassigned

Cases with nobody responsible — the list that turns into a missed deadline if nobody reads it.

Outcomes

Appeals with a decision recorded, and covered days or amounts recovered where they were captured.

From the organization down to one case

Organization → region/group → facility → appeal. Narrow to a region or a single building without telephoning every location to ask.

Access follows your structure

A regional director sees their region. A group director sees their line of business across regions. An executive sees the whole portfolio in figures, with no individual resident named. Each level can grant access only inside what it is responsible for itself, which is set on screen rather than by a request to us.

What is not there yet

Overturn rate by health plan, and the share of eligible notices that never became an appeal, are not built. Both need data the product does not yet capture completely, and neither is claimed anywhere on these pages.
