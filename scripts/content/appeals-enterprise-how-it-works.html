From a Medicare notice
to a prepared appeal.

The same process in every building.

CaseWhy Appeals is not affiliated with or endorsed by Medicare, CMS, or any health plan, and is not a law firm.

Four steps

  1. Upload the notice. Photograph or upload the Medicare notice the facility received.
  2. Identify the appeal. CaseWhy reads it, takes the dates and the denial reason, and works out which appeal applies and by when.
  3. Prepare it. The materials appropriate to that notice, with the rule cited and the dates already filled in.
  4. Manage the case. Assign an owner, track the deadline, and see it on the portfolio board.

The two situations are not the same appeal

A stay cut short

A NOMNC starts a fast-track review, and it is a telephone call to the BFCC-QIO before noon the day before coverage ends. CaseWhy identifies the right BFCC-QIO and its number, calculates the applicable deadlines, and prepares a script with the dates, the regulation and what to ask for.

A denied admission

This goes in writing to the plan that issued the denial, on a different clock. CaseWhy prepares the written appeal with the applicable regulatory references, and the CMS-1696 appointment-of-representative form for completion and authorization where appropriate.

What the facility team gets

  • Deadlines computed from the notice by rule, rather than written by an AI.
  • One board showing every case the facility has open, its stage and its next date.
  • Call scripts, regulatory references and representative authorization forms.
  • No resident names or Medicare numbers required in the facility case record.

CaseWhy prepares and tracks appeals. It does not submit them on your behalf, and it does not decide them — those belong to your team and to the reviewing body.
