Open an account

Pick the level you are responsible for. Each one opens a real account; what differs is how much of the organization it covers.

One facility

A single building. The business office, social services or nursing staff can set it up themselves — it does not need a director or an owner — and nothing starts until your first letter.

Open an account →

A group

One care setting across the organization — your skilled nursing buildings, say, with home health kept separate. A group director sees that setting wherever it operates.

Open an account →

A region

The buildings in one region, however you draw it. A regional director sees their own region and nothing else, which is what makes it workable for one region to start before the rest.

Open an account →

Enterprise

Every facility in the organization, with regions and care settings mapped to your own structure. An executive sees the whole portfolio in figures, with no individual resident named.

Open an account →

All four open a real account straight away. A single facility is usable as soon as you are signed in; a group, a region or an enterprise then needs its structure mapped — which regions, which care settings, and who sees across them — and that is done with you rather than asked for in a form.

Before you decide

  • Price your portfolio — the calculator gives you a monthly figure for your own footprint, with no form and nobody to ask.
  • See the portfolio view — what leadership actually sees across every building.
  • The free 30-day pilot — what the thirty days cover, and what happens at the end of them.

Non-profit facilities

A non-profit skilled nursing facility is not charged, and is never quoted a price. That applies at any of the levels above, and such locations are not counted toward the portfolio band — counting them would hand a portfolio a discount earned by facilities nobody pays for.
