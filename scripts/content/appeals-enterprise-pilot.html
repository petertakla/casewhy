How it works

  1. Pick the facilities. One building, or several.
  2. Let the team use it. The people who already handle Medicare notices — social services, the business office, nursing.
  3. Run real cases. Not sample data. Your own notices, your own deadlines.
  4. Look at what happened. Cases prepared, deadlines met, and what the staff thought.
  5. Decide. Extend it to more buildings, or stop.

What the free period is

  • Thirty days, free.
  • No card, and no charge during or after it.
  • The thirty days start with your first generated letter, not when you register.
  • Nothing converts to a paid plan on its own, and there is nothing to cancel.

A non-profit skilled nursing facility is not charged at all, and is never quoted a price — so for them there is no period to start and nothing to decide at the end of it.
