What every case includes

  • Every appeal level on that case for 12 months, including a second or third round if it goes further.
  • The portfolio dashboard, for everyone your structure gives access to.
  • Deadline tracking, case assignment, and appeal preparation.
  • No charge for a location that files nothing in a month — you pay for cases, not for seats or sites.

A single location

One building has nothing to pool with, so it buys on its own: pay per case with no commitment, or take a monthly plan with cases included. Portfolio rates start at two locations.

{{PRICING:facility:snf}}

These are the single-location rates. A portfolio is quoted against its actual mix of care settings, which is what the calculator above does.

Non-profit facilities

A non-profit skilled nursing facility is not charged, and is never quoted a price. That is a standing commitment rather than a discount, and such locations are not counted toward the portfolio band — counting them would hand a portfolio a discount earned by facilities nobody pays for.
