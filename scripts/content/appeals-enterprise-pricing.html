The discount is earned by the whole portfolio and applied to every facility in it, whichever care settings they are. A twelve-month agreement applies to portfolio pricing.

What the monthly price includes

  • Unlimited appeals at that facility — there is no per-case charge on a monthly plan.
  • The portfolio dashboard, for everyone your structure gives access to.
  • Deadline tracking, case assignment, and appeal preparation.

Pay per case instead

A facility that handles a handful of appeals a year can pay $25 a case for skilled nursing rather than a monthly fee, with no commitment. Portfolio discounts apply to the monthly plans; a mixed portfolio is quoted against its actual mix.

Non-profit facilities

A non-profit skilled nursing facility is not charged, and is never quoted a price. That is a standing commitment rather than a discount, and such locations are not counted toward the portfolio band — counting them would hand a portfolio a discount earned by facilities nobody pays for.
