Managing Medicare appeals across multiple facilities should not mean spreadsheets, forwarded email, and a different process in every building.

CaseWhy Appeals gives leadership one view of Medicare appeal activity across skilled nursing operations — which cases need attention, who is responsible, and where a deadline is close — from the organization down to the individual facility.

“Every Medicare appeal” means the appeal types CaseWhy covers today: Medicare Advantage denials, Part D drug denials, Original Medicare claim denials, premiums and late-enrolment penalties, enrollment and disenrollment, and PACE.

The enterprise problem

Individual facilities may each have a working process. What an operator usually lacks is a consistent view across all of them.

Missed deadlines

A fast-track appeal of a coverage termination is a phone call before noon the day before. An opportunity can pass before leadership knows it existed.

Fragmented accountability

Business offices, social workers and administrators all take part. Without one record of who owns the next action, cases sit.

No portfolio view

Twenty buildings, twenty processes. Nothing says which of them needs help this week.

What CaseWhy does about it

  • Appeal activity across every participating facility and region, in one board.
  • Deadlines surfaced before they pass, calculated from the notice.
  • A named owner for every case, and a list of the ones with nobody on them.
  • One way of preparing an appeal, the same in every building.
  • Access that follows your structure — a regional director sees their region, and nothing else.
