Medicare Advantage plans can deny your residents at two points. Pick the one in front of you.

Admission

The plan refused the admission before the resident arrived. Your facility appeals as the resident's appointed representative (CMS-1696), starting with the plan's own reconsideration. You have 60 days from the denial notice, 65 counting mail time.

Of the denials that were appealed, 95% were overturned. Only 18% of denials were appealed.

Mid-stay

The plan is ending a resident's covered stay. The resident gets a NOMNC. The fast appeal goes to the independent QIO and has to be requested by noon the day before coverage ends.

Reviewers disagreed with the plan's decision roughly 49% of the time across 625,608 fast-track appeals.
