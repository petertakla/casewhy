For home health agencies, hospices & independent SNFs

A family that wins a fast appeal keeps the service you're providing running while the appeal is decided — that's good for the family, and it's good for you. CaseWhy Appeals explains the notice, writes the fast-appeal request, and tracks the two-day clock.

CaseWhy Appeals is not affiliated with or endorsed by Medicare, CMS, or any health plan, and is not a law firm.

Why a successful fast appeal is in your interest

When a Notice of Medicare Non-Coverage goes out and a family doesn't appeal — or appeals late, or appeals without the right facts — the service stops. A family that understands the notice, knows the deadline, and files on time is a family whose care continues without a gap.

Hospice is pay-per-case only.
