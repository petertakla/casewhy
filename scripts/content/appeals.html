CaseWhy Appeals — every Medicare appeal, explained, written, and tracked

CaseWhy Appeals is the Medicare appeals engine that works alongside what you already use — your practice software, your CRM, your own spreadsheet. It doesn't replace your system of record; it does the appeal. Every appeal type it covers is listed below.

CaseWhy Appeals is not affiliated with or endorsed by Medicare, CMS, or any health plan, and is not a law firm.

What a family sees

  • Explain it. What the notice actually says, in plain language, with the rule behind it cited.
  • Write it. The appeal letter and the filled forms, ready to send.
  • Keep track of it. Both deadlines computed, a step-by-step tracker, and reminders that don't stop at one letter.

What a professional gets

  • Letters that cite the rule, with a checkable citation chip on every claim — never a bare assertion.
  • Both clocks computed automatically: the filing deadline and the decider's own deadline, for every level from the plan up through the Medicare Appeals Council.
  • The CMS forms for the case's level filled, not just described — including the Appointment of Representative form (CMS-1696) naming you.
  • An Appeal Tracker per case: the numbered steps, due dates, and what's done.
  • Letters exported on your own organization's letterhead.
  • Every deadline into your calendar by an ICS feed — one subscription covers every client.
  • Your caseload imported in one pass. A CSV from your own system — Clio, LegalServer, a spreadsheet, whatever you already use — mapped to label, reference and tags, then matched and updated by reference on every re-import rather than duplicated.
  • One organization, every seat sharing it. Invite your whole team; clients belong to the organization rather than the person who typed them in, and reminders route to whoever's case it is.
  • Covers every appeal type — Medicare Advantage, Part D, Original Medicare, premiums (IRMAA and late-enrollment penalties), enrollment, and PACE.
  • The next level is already drafted. When a case is upheld, the next level's steps and letter are readied automatically — the tracker doesn't stop at one round.
  • The resource library once your seat is live: written guides, a video walkthrough and audio explainers, so your team is running on it fast.
  • Export anytime. A CSV of every client and case by reference, and every letter and form downloadable from the case itself — nothing of yours is held hostage here.
  • No client names stored. A client is a label and a reference number you choose — never a name, never a Medicare number.

Why this exists

"We built CaseWhy Appeals because our own family's Medicare letters didn't explain themselves — a denial would arrive, and figuring out whether it was even worth appealing, and by when, took hours we didn't have. Then we learned the real number: only 1 in 9 Medicare Advantage denials gets appealed, but more than 8 in 10 appeals win. That gap is the appeal paradox, and it's the reason this exists — not because appealing is hard to win, but because almost nobody does it." — Peter, founder of CaseWhy
