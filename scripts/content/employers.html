Help us build CaseWhy for your sponsored employees

CaseWhy already helps individuals track and understand their own USCIS case, free. We're exploring a team feature for employers sponsoring visas or green cards — a way for HR or mobility teams to see aggregate status across sponsored employees, without ever seeing an individual case unless that employee explicitly consents.

What we're considering

  • A consent-based team dashboard — an employer never sees a sponsored employee's case unless that employee opts in.
  • A shared deadline calendar across sponsored cases.
  • Stalled-case alerts surfaced to the team, not just the individual.
  • Visa bulletin movement tracked across your whole sponsored population.
  • A plain-language digest your HR team can actually read.

None of this exists yet — these are planned capabilities we're validating, not live features.
