Your students ask you what "Case Was Received" means, how long an OPT card takes, and whether a quiet case is a problem. CaseWhy answers those questions for free, in plain language, in English and Spanish — so you can point to it instead of explaining USCIS's status language for the hundredth time.

For your students

  • Status explained. Every USCIS status in plain language, no account required.
  • OPT and STEM OPT processing times. USCIS's published I-765 figures with the capture date, and what "outside normal processing time" means for an I-765 (see processing times).
  • Travel documents. What an I-131 advance parole application covers and the abandonment risk of leaving while an adjustment is pending (read the I-131 memo).
  • Who to ask. Free directories of accredited representatives, legal aid, and attorneys, plus a free no-sign-in question box for "what does this mean" questions. CaseWhy never gives case-specific advice; for that it points students to a licensed attorney or accredited representative.
